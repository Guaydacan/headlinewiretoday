Giants Lose Dart for the Season as Winston Takes Over Against Tennessee

By Headline Wire Today Staff Published April 6, 2026 6 min read

New York will start Jameis Winston against Tennessee on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET, and the Giants will play the rest of the regular season without the rookie they handed the offense to. Jaxson Dart is on injured reserve after hurting his knee on the seventh play from scrimmage in Monday's 28-6 loss at the Rams (MCL, PCL and meniscus damage, with the ACL intact).

The picture hardened over two days. Harbaugh said Monday night the team had probably avoided "a disastrous outcome," then pulled back Tuesday: "I don't want to get out over our skis on that." That day's imaging looked worse, and by Wednesday it was season-ending.

Winston takes over after going 11 of 27 for 111 yards with an interception in relief Monday. "This is what I've always dreamed of, to be a starting quarterback," he said. "Obviously, my respects and gratitude is just going to be with Jaxson, but at the same time, this is what I dream of." The only other passer on hand is practice squad quarterback Jake Haener. A CBS Sports column Thursday floated a Joe Flacco trade on the Harbaugh reunion angle before arguing against it, but Flacco is Cincinnati's backup and nothing beyond speculation has been reported. Brian Burns (ankle) and Andrew Thomas (groin) are questionable, while Malik Nabers practiced fully after hurting a shoulder Monday.

First, though, Atlanta visits Green Bay tonight at 8:15 p.m. ET on Amazon Prime, where Michael Penix Jr. makes his season debut after knee surgery for an 0-2 Falcons team that has scored 16 points with seven turnovers. Green Bay is without Jayden Reed (neck) and Micah Parsons (knee, PUP).

Quarterback questions in Chicago and Washington

Chicago may open Monday night against Philadelphia with its third-string quarterback. Caleb Williams (hamstring) is doubtful after sitting out Wednesday and Thursday, hurt in the fourth quarter of Sunday's 9-3 loss to Minnesota, and Tyson Bagent entered concussion protocol Tuesday and did not practice Thursday. That leaves Case Keenum, the only healthy and available signal-caller on the active roster and the likely starter. Neither has been ruled out, and because the Bears play Monday the call lands late. Williams has never missed a game as a pro.

Washington starts Marcus Mariota against Seattle on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET. Jayden Daniels is out with a dislocated left elbow, and the history is the concern: he dislocated the same elbow in Week 9 last season against Seattle and reinjured it in Week 14, ending his 2025. He was carted off in Sunday's 37-20 loss at Dallas, and X-rays showed no fracture. He is projected back next week, though that is a projection rather than a commitment.

Buffalo, 2-0 after a 41-31 win over Detroit, is down to three healthy wide receivers for the Chargers' visit Sunday at 1 p.m. ET on FOX. DJ Moore (shoulder) was limited Wednesday and then a full non-participant Thursday, and Keon Coleman (ankle) also missed Thursday, leaving Khalil Shakir, Joshua Palmer and Skyler Bell. Both are Friday decisions.

Dallas flies 5,220 miles to Rio with no bye waiting

Baltimore and Dallas meet Sunday at 4:25 p.m. ET at the Maracana in Rio de Janeiro, and the schedule around it has no precedent. Dallas is the first team in league history to fly at least 5,000 miles to a game and not get a bye afterward: about 5,220 miles each way, then a road game at Houston next week. Baltimore flies 4,781 and at least comes home.

The closest comparisons are Denver's London game last season and Miami's trip in 2021. Both teams won the game they traveled for, and both trailed by double digits deep into the fourth quarter the following week. Dallas coach Brian Schottenheimer says the plan is rest: "We're going to fly through the night. It's going to be a dark plane. We'll feed them a little bit, but we need them to rest."

Dallas is without DeMarvion Overshown and Malik Hooker. Baltimore lists Zay Flowers (hamstring), Trey Hendrickson (finger) and Ronnie Stanley (toe) as questionable after all three missed Wednesday, and losing Flowers or Hendrickson would matter most.

The rest of the injury report

Saquon Barkley is questionable for Monday night after leaving Sunday's game at Tennessee with a stinger on the first offensive snap. Philadelphia practiced for the first time this week Thursday, and Barkley worked in a cowboy collar, a molded foam device that stops the head from snapping backward. He said his MRI "went well."

Puka Nacua is questionable for the Rams' Sunday night game at Denver with a groin injury, after missing Monday's win over the Giants and sitting out Wednesday. Reporting Thursday pointed to little progress and a possible second straight absence, though McVay said it is not a hernia and not long-term. Davante Adams went for 195 yards and two touchdowns as the top target in his place.

Indianapolis hosts Houston at 1 p.m. ET without Alec Pierce, whose heel injury is a four-game absence rather than a week-to-week question, with a return expected around Week 7.

If you follow one of these teams, the last word comes late. Friday's reports settle Moore, Coleman, Nacua, Barkley and Flowers, Chicago's quarterback will not be official until Saturday, and four 0-2 teams (Washington, Miami, Atlanta and Tennessee) are already fighting history, since only 12 percent of teams starting 0-2 since 2002 have reached the playoffs.
