Six Ranked Matchups Land on One Saturday and the Injury Reports Are Still Moving

By Headline Wire Today Staff Published April 6, 2026 6 min read

This Saturday is the first genuinely stacked weekend of the season. Six games put a ranked team against another ranked team, three of them are priced inside a field goal, and the availability reports in half of those matchups were still changing on Wednesday night.

The one everybody will start with kicks at noon Eastern. No. 1 Texas goes to No. 14 Tennessee, and both teams walk in at 3-0. The oddity is the venue: these two programs have never played on either campus, and they have not met anywhere since the Cotton Bowl on New Year's Day in 1969. Texas is favored by four, which for a road team walking into Neyland at noon is a narrow number.

Tennessee's injury situation is the thing worth watching before kickoff. The Vols released their first availability report of the season on Wednesday night, and five players are already ruled out, including a safety, a tight end, a linebacker and two more defensive backs. A running back and a defensive lineman are listed as questionable after both sat out last week.

The name that is not on the report is the one that matters most. Their leading rusher was shaken up in the second quarter last week, grabbing at his left side after a tackle, and came out for good in the third. He is absent from the injury list entirely, which points to him playing. Two more reports come out Thursday and Friday, and the final one lands ninety minutes before kickoff.

The rest of the day is just as tight. No. 4 Ole Miss goes to No. 21 Florida, both unbeaten, with Florida favored by three and a half at home. Ole Miss has a running back carrying a shoulder problem who is questionable for it. No. 17 Iowa travels to No. 18 Michigan, both 3-0, Michigan by five and a half. No. 19 Missouri at No. 24 Mississippi State is another pairing of unbeatens, and Mississippi State will be without a defensive lineman who is done for the season.

At night it gets better. No. 20 Oregon at No. 12 USC is the pick of the evening: USC is the only team in the group at 4-0, Oregon is favored by three on the road, and the Ducks will be without a receiver who broke his foot in warmups last week and is out indefinitely. No. 23 Texas A&M at No. 10 LSU closes it out, with LSU favored by eight and a half and a tight end whose return has no timetable.

Around those six, the top of the rankings gets a lighter afternoon. No. 2 Georgia hosts Oklahoma as a two-touchdown favorite, though Oklahoma may be down a defensive lineman who left practice Tuesday with a lower-body injury. No. 3 Notre Dame goes to Purdue laying nearly four touchdowns. No. 7 Ohio State hosts Illinois. No. 8 Alabama hosts South Carolina and gets a receiver and a running back back in the lineup. No. 13 Penn State hosts Wisconsin with a receiver questionable.

The useful thing about a weekend shaped like this one is that it settles arguments. Ten of the teams playing Saturday are unbeaten, and by Sunday morning at least five of them will not be. Three of the six ranked matchups are close enough on paper that the availability report ninety minutes before kickoff may matter more than anything either team did in September.

If you are picking one to watch, the noon window has the biggest name and the strangest history, the evening has the better matchup on paper, and the SEC games in between have the most bodies missing. Check the final injury reports before you commit to any of them.
